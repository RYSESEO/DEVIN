How it works
Full intelligence in one call

GET /v1/service/{domain} returns billing, contacts, legal flags, and every lifecycle path.

Request
GET /v1/service/netflix.com
X-API-Key: ck_live_...

// Or query specific paths:
GET /v1/service/netflix.com/path?type=pause
GET /v1/billing/netflix.com
GET /v1/contact/netflix.com
GET /v1/signals/netflix.com

{
  "domain": "netflix.com",

    "model": "auto_renew",

Capabilities
Not just cancellation data

One integration powers every subscription lifecycle feature your product needs.

Full Lifecycle Paths

Cancel, pause, downgrade, refund, and account delete. Structured step-by-step instructions with URLs, selectors, phone numbers, and confidence scores. 142 services across 22 categories, from Netflix to AT&T to HelloFresh.

Billing Intelligence

Billing model, trial policies, refund windows, auto-renewal detection. Know what users are paying and when it renews.

Support Contacts

Phone numbers, chat URLs, email addresses, business hours, and expected hold times for every service.

Legal & Compliance

State-specific auto-renewal law flags, ETF disclosures, FTC ROSCA, and in-person-required indicators per service.

Churn Signals & Retention Intelligence

Retention offer intelligence, churn difficulty scoring, and recommended actions. Know what discount the service will likely offer, how hard they'll push, and whether to pause instead of cancel. Trained on real retention patterns across 142+ services.

API Surface
Clean REST, structured responses

Every endpoint returns confidence scores, last-verified timestamps, and complexity scores.

GET /v1/service/{domain} Full intelligence: billing + contacts + all paths
GET /v1/service/{domain}/path?type= Specific lifecycle path (cancel, pause, etc.)
GET /v1/billing/{domain} Billing model, trial & refund policy
GET /v1/contact/{domain} Support channels, hold times, hours
GET /v1/signals/{domain} Retention offers, churn risk, actions
GET /v1/cancel/{domain} Cancel-only (backwards-compatible)
GET /v1/services All services (paginated, filterable)
POST /v1/report Flag broken or outdated paths
POST /v1/keys Create a free API key
